## The image

A newspaper front page — masthead with the date, one big lead story with a
drawn scene, then the rest of the day's stories in a clean grid:

- **Lead:** COP31 collapses with no deal. Two weeks of talks and two days
  of overtime end in Antalya with no agreement: the EU rejects a final
  draft with no mention of fossil fuels, and the presidency declares no
  consensus — the longest climate summit ever.
- **Iran strikes US bases in Bahrain** — the IRGC claims a missile-and-drone
  attack on Al-Riffa and Awali, saying it hit two Patriot radars; Bahrain
  says a civilian industrial site burned and two people were hurt; the US
  denies an F-16 was hit.
- **Mexico captures 'El Chapo Isidro'** — special forces seize Fausto Isidro
  Meza Flores, an FBI top-10 fugitive, in Sinaloa; eleven killed, including
  four soldiers.
- **Missile hits Kryvyi Rih; EU–Indonesia sign a landmark trade deal; S&P 500
  in correction; a factory fire kills 3 in Indonesia; NATO vows to defend
  'every inch' of Poland; a boat capsizes in Nigeria** — plus Houthi fire on
  Saudi Arabia and UN killer-robot talks ending with no binding deal.

All text is drawn with PIL + DejaVu fonts; the Antalya dusk scene is a
flat vector-style drawing made with PIL primitives. No AI-rendered text
anywhere, so nothing can come out garbled.
